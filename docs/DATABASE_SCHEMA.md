# PRAVAH Database Schema

## 1. Blood Banks

Stores information about blood banks connected to the PRAVAH network.

Fields:
- bank_id
- name
- city
- latitude
- longitude
- capacity
- status

---

## 2. Inventory

Stores blood component inventory.

Fields:
- inventory_id
- bank_id
- component
- blood_group
- quantity
- collection_date
- expiry_date
- status

---

## 3. Usage History

Stores historical blood/component usage.

Fields:
- usage_id
- bank_id
- component
- blood_group
- date
- units_used

---

## 4. Cold Chain

Stores temperature and handling information.

Fields:
- telemetry_id
- bank_id
- timestamp
- temperature
- agitation_status

---

## 5. Equipment

Stores blood-storage equipment information.

Fields:
- equipment_id
- bank_id
- equipment_type
- health_score
- status

---

## 6. Demand Forecast

Stores predictions generated by the demand forecasting model.

Fields:
- forecast_id
- bank_id
- component
- blood_group
- forecast_date
- predicted_demand
- model_version

---

## 7. Risk Prediction

Stores expiry-risk predictions generated by the expiry-risk model.

Fields:
- risk_id
- inventory_id
- risk_score
- risk_level
- contributing_features
- model_version
- created_at

---

## 8. Transfer Recommendation

Stores recommended blood transfers.

Fields:
- transfer_id
- source_bank
- destination_bank
- component
- blood_group
- quantity
- route
- vehicle
- status
- created_at

---

## 9. Audit Log

Stores important actions and approval decisions.

Fields:
- audit_id
- timestamp
- action
- user
- recommendation
- source
- destination
- quantity
- approval_status
